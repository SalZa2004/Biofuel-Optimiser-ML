CNthesizer

Data-Driven Optimisation of Compression Ignition Fuel

CNthesizer is an integrated computational platform developed to support the efficient exploration of compression ignition fuel design space. Motivated by the continued importance of diesel engines in transport and industry, and the need to reduce emissions from conventional fossil fuels, the platform combines molecular data, machine learning property prediction, mixture and biodiesel analysis, fuel constraint screening, optimisation, and generative molecular design within a single workflow.

The tool focuses on key fuel properties that govern ignition behaviour, combustion performance, emissions potential, and practical fuel suitability, including cetane number, yield sooting index, boiling point, density, viscosity, and lower heating value.

Projected oil demand highlights the continued importance of diesel in non-OECD markets.

Why CI Fuels?

Compression ignition engines remain important in transport and industry because of their high efficiency, durability, and reliability under demanding operating conditions.

Why Cleaner Fuels?

Conventional diesel fuels contribute to greenhouse gas emissions and particulate matter formation. Cleaner alternative fuels and additives are therefore needed to reduce environmental impact.

Why Machine Learning?

Experimental fuel testing is expensive and time-consuming. Machine learning enables rapid prediction and screening of large molecular and mixture design spaces.

CNthesizer Functional Modules

The platform is organised into three main functional modules: property prediction, optimisation, and generation. Each module contains sub-tools that support fuel evaluation, blend analysis, candidate screening, and molecular design.

Property Prediction

Predict key fuel properties from molecular structure or fuel composition.

Pure Fuel Property Predictor

Predict DCN, YSI, boiling point, density, viscosity, and lower heating value from IUPAC name or SMILES.

Biodiesel CN Predictor

Predict biodiesel cetane number from FAME composition and engineered composition features.

Mixture CN Predictor

Estimate mixture fuel properties using blend components and composition fractions.

Optimisation

Screen and optimise candidate fuels based on target performance constraints.

Screening

Apply fuel property constraints to identify candidates that satisfy operational CI engine requirements.

Binary Blending

Explore binary blend compositions and identify promising blend ratios for target fuel properties.

Generation

Generate new molecular candidates and blend components for fuel design.

Pure Fuel Generator

Use molecular evolution to generate pure fuel candidates targeting high CN, low YSI, or specified CN values.

Blend Component Generator

Generate possible additives or blend components to improve fuel performance and composition suitability.

Research Gap

Existing machine learning studies often focus on predicting properties of pure compounds. However, real compression ignition fuels are usually complex blends, and fuel design requires balancing multiple competing objectives such as high cetane number and low sooting tendency.

Project Contribution

CNthesizer addresses this gap by integrating pure fuel prediction, mixture modelling, biodiesel FAME prediction, fuel constraint screening, and generative molecule design within a single computational tool.

Functional Workflow

The GUI follows the same logic as the project methodology: users provide molecular or composition inputs, select a module, and receive predictions, optimised blends, or generated candidates.

User Inputs → Property Prediction → Optimisation → Generation → Candidate Outputs
