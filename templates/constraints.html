Fuel Property Constraints

Fuel Constraints Applied in CNthesizer

The following constraints are used to assess whether predicted fuel properties fall within acceptable operational limits for compression-ignition (CI) engine applications.

Pure Component Constraints
No. Constraint
1 Cetane number must satisfy a minimum threshold, CN ≥ 40, to ensure acceptable ignition quality.
2 Yield Sooting Index must remain below a defined limit, YSI ≤ 50, to control particulate emissions.
3 Density must satisfy a minimum threshold, ρ ≥ 720 kg m−3, for compatibility with CI engines.
4 Dynamic viscosity must remain below a defined limit, μ ≤ 2.0 mPa s, to ensure proper fuel injection behaviour.
5 Boiling point must lie within a specified range, 60°C ≤ Tb ≤ 250°C, for compatibility with CI engines.
6 Lower heating value must satisfy a minimum threshold, LHV ≥ 30 MJ kg−1, to maintain sufficient energy content.
Mixture Constraints
No. Constraint
7 Cetane number must satisfy a minimum threshold, CN ≥ 51, consistent with the BS EN 590 standard.
8 Density must lie within the range, 820 ≤ ρ ≤ 845 kg m−3, consistent with the BS EN 590 standard.
9 Boiling point must fall within the range, 180°C ≤ Tb ≤ 360°C, consistent with the BS EN 590 standard.
